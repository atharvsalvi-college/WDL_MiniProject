A few minutes of preparation can help

☀
01Before you head outPlan around the hottest hours

Check the local forecast and official heat alerts. Reschedule strenuous outdoor work away from the hottest part of the day where possible, and carry water.

02While it is hotCool down and check on others

Choose shade or a cooler indoor place, take breaks, and drink water regularly. Check on older adults, young children, outdoor workers, and anyone who may need extra support.

03Recognize an emergencyAct quickly if someone becomes very ill

Confusion, fainting, seizures, or loss of consciousness can be signs of a heat emergency. Move the person to a cooler place, begin cooling them, and contact local emergency medical services. Do not give fluids to someone who is unconscious.
