About the Heatwave Intelligence System

Objective

The Heatwave Intelligence System is designed to monitor, predict, and report heatwave conditions. It helps citizens, authorities, and emergency services make informed decisions during extreme weather events.

Data Sources

Weather information is collected from the India Meteorological Department (IMD), Automatic Weather Stations (AWS), satellite observations, and local environmental monitoring systems.

Stakeholders

The system supports government agencies, disaster management teams, healthcare departments, local authorities, and the general public in responding effectively to heatwave situations.

Message from the Chief Meteorologist

"Heatwaves can have a significant impact on health and daily life. Citizens are advised to stay inside, avoid prolonged exposure to direct sunlight, and follow official weather advisories to remain safe during extreme heat conditions."
